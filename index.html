01

Tsunami Relief Dribble

In January 2012 I dribbled a basketball 370 km (230 miles) from Shibuya, Tokyo to Sendai in 12 days, through snow and cold, collecting messages of support for Tohoku. More than 200 people welcomed me at the Sendai City Gymnasium, where I handed the ball to the Sendai 89ers before their home game. The donations, including $5,700 (457,000 JPY) from 56 supporters online, went to a program that gives basketballs to junior high schools in Miyagi. Reuters carried the story around the world.

03

Free Throw Distraction Project

At the Oklahoma City Thunder in 2010–11, I co-developed a data-driven initiative to make our arena the toughest environment in the NBA for visiting free-throw shooters. We benchmarked all 30 arenas on visitor free-throw performance (OKC ranked 14th), reviewed academic research and the Dallas Mavericks' ThunderStix test, and designed a scripted in-arena activation for late-game moments. I presented the proposal to department leadership. A modern dashboard version is next.

Riceballs for the team

At the Oklahoma City Thunder I made 20 to 30 riceballs before every home game, playoffs included, about 50 times in all, for the entertainment staff and the Storm Chasers. The Director of Entertainment loved them and asked me to keep going all season.
